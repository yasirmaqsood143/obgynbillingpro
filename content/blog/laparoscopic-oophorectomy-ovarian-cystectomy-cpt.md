Adnexal surgery — removing an ovary, a tube, a cyst or part of an ovary — is some of the most common gynecologic surgery performed, and some of the most frequently miscoded. The right code depends on the **approach** (laparoscopic or open), **what was removed** (whole ovary, part of the ovary, a cyst, a tube), and **whether the procedure was unilateral or bilateral**. This guide walks through each scenario.

## Adnexal surgery codes at a glance

| Procedure | Laparoscopic | Open |
|---|---|---|
| Oophorectomy (partial or total), with or without salpingectomy | `58661` | `58940` (oophorectomy); `58720` (salpingo-oophorectomy) |
| Salpingectomy (complete or partial) | `58661` | `58700` |
| Excision/fulguration of lesions of ovary or pelvic viscera (e.g., cystectomy, endometrioma excision) | `58662` | `58925` (ovarian cystectomy) |
| Wedge resection or bisection of ovary | — (consider `58661`/`58662` or unlisted per documentation) | `58920` |
| Lysis of adhesions | `58660` | `58740` |
| Tubal sterilization | `58670` / `58671` | `58600`, `58605`, `58611` (at cesarean) |
| Diagnostic laparoscopy | `49320` (included in surgical laparoscopy) | — |

Robotic-assisted procedures use the laparoscopic codes. See [CPT code for robotic hysterectomy](/blog/cpt-code-robotic-hysterectomy) for how robotic assistance and HCPCS `S2900` are handled.

## Laparoscopic oophorectomy: 58661

`58661` describes **laparoscopy, surgical; with removal of adnexal structures (partial or total oophorectomy and/or salpingectomy)**. It covers:

- Laparoscopic unilateral or bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (when no hysterectomy is performed)
- Laparoscopic oophorectomy without salpingectomy
- Laparoscopic salpingectomy (for example, for hydrosalpinx or opportunistic salpingectomy as a stand-alone procedure)
- Partial oophorectomy

**Bilateral procedures:** Medicare assigns `58661` a bilateral surgery indicator of **1**, meaning bilateral payment rules apply and modifier `50` is used when the procedure is performed on both sides. Some commercial payers follow guidance to report `58661` once regardless of laterality. Check each payer's policy and use laterality modifiers (`RT`, `LT`) where a payer requires them.

**With hysterectomy:** don't report `58661` with a laparoscopic hysterectomy code that already includes "removal of tube(s) and/or ovary(s)" (`58571`, `58573`, `58542`, `58544`, `58552`, `58554`).

## Laparoscopic ovarian cystectomy: 58662 or 58661?

This is the most debated adnexal coding question. Ovarian cystectomy — shelling out a cyst while preserving the ovary — doesn't have a dedicated laparoscopic code. Common practice:

- **`58662`** (laparoscopy with fulguration or excision of lesions of the ovary, pelvic viscera or peritoneal surface by any method) when the cyst or lesion is **excised and the ovary preserved**. This is the approach many coding resources recommend for ovarian cystectomy, including endometrioma excision.
- **`58661`** when **part of the ovary itself** is removed (partial oophorectomy) or the whole ovary is removed.

The operative note should state clearly whether ovarian tissue was removed, or only the cyst wall and contents. Payers that disagree with the code choice will usually want to see that description.

## Open procedures: 58940, 58720, 58925 and 58920

- **`58940`** — oophorectomy, partial or total, unilateral or bilateral (open)
- **`58720`** — salpingo-oophorectomy, complete or partial, unilateral or bilateral (open)
- **`58925`** — ovarian cystectomy, unilateral or bilateral (open)
- **`58920`** — wedge resection or bisection of ovary, unilateral or bilateral (open)

Because these descriptors include "unilateral or bilateral," they're reported once regardless of laterality — no modifier `50`.

## Ectopic pregnancy

Laparoscopic treatment of tubal ectopic pregnancy has its own codes: `59150` (without salpingectomy and/or oophorectomy) and `59151` (with salpingectomy and/or oophorectomy). Don't report `58661` for an ectopic pregnancy salpingectomy.

## Adnexal torsion

Laparoscopic detorsion of the ovary without removal has no specific code; it's commonly reported with an unlisted laparoscopy code or, per some payer guidance, another code that reflects the work. If the ovary is removed, `58661` applies.

## Diagnosis coding

| ICD-10-CM | Description |
|---|---|
| `N83.0-` | Follicular cyst of ovary (by laterality) |
| `N83.1-` | Corpus luteum cyst (by laterality) |
| `N83.2-` | Other and unspecified ovarian cysts (including `N83.20-`, `N83.29-`) |
| `N83.5-` | Torsion of ovary, ovarian pedicle and fallopian tube |
| `N80.1-` | Endometriosis of ovary (including endometrioma) |
| `D27.-` | Benign neoplasm of ovary (e.g., dermoid/mature teratoma), by laterality |
| `N70.-` | Salpingitis and oophoritis |
| `N73.-` | Other female pelvic inflammatory diseases |
| `Z40.02` | Encounter for prophylactic removal of ovary(s) |
| `Z15.0-` / `Z80.41` | Genetic susceptibility / family history supporting risk-reducing surgery |

Code to the **pathology**: a cyst presumed to be functional that turns out to be a dermoid should be coded `D27.-`, not `N83.2-`. Risk-reducing salpingo-oophorectomy needs the prophylactic and risk-factor codes to support medical necessity.

## Combining adnexal procedures

- **Cystectomy on one side, oophorectomy on the other** — `58662` and `58661` may both be reportable with a distinct-procedure modifier, if NCCI edits allow and documentation clearly separates the procedures by side. Review edits carefully.
- **Endometriosis excision plus oophorectomy** — often bundled when in the same area; separate reporting needs clearly distinct sites.
- **Lysis of adhesions** — generally bundled with other laparoscopic procedures; consider modifier `22` on the primary procedure if adhesiolysis substantially increased the work.
- **Diagnostic laparoscopy** — never separately reported with a surgical laparoscopy.

## Worked examples

**1. Laparoscopic right salpingo-oophorectomy for a complex cyst; pathology shows a mature cystic teratoma.**
`58661` (with `RT` if the payer requires laterality) · `D27.0` (benign neoplasm of right ovary)

**2. Robotic left ovarian cystectomy for an endometrioma, ovary preserved.**
`58662` · `N80.1-` (endometriosis of ovary, with the specific code for the documented laterality and depth)

**3. Laparoscopic bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy for risk reduction in a BRCA1 carrier, uterus left in place.**
`58661-50` for Medicare (or per payer policy) · `Z40.02` · `Z15.01` (genetic susceptibility to malignant neoplasm of breast) and/or `Z15.02` (ovary) as documented · family history codes as applicable

**4. Open left oophorectomy for a large ovarian mass.**
`58940` · diagnosis per pathology

## Documentation checklist

- Approach (laparoscopic, robotic-assisted, open; any conversion)
- Exactly what was removed from **each** side — whole ovary, part of ovary, cyst only, tube
- Whether the ovary was preserved
- Any additional procedures and their locations
- Pathology results for final diagnosis coding
