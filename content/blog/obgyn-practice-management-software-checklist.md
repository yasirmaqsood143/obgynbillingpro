Whether you're opening a new practice, outgrowing a basic system, or preparing for the 2027 maternity code changes, choosing practice management (PM) software is a decision you'll live with for years. Demos are designed to impress. This checklist keeps the focus on what matters to your revenue: how the system handles OB/GYN billing day to day.

## Before the demo: know what you need

Write down:

- Number of providers (physicians, midwives, NPs) and locations
- Monthly visit, delivery and surgery volumes
- Top payers and whether you bill Medicaid managed care
- Whether billing will be in-house, outsourced or a mix
- Must-have integrations (lab, ultrasound, hospital, patient portal, payments)
- Your budget for software, clearinghouse and implementation

Send the vendor two or three real (de-identified) scenarios in advance and ask them to walk through those, not their standard script.

## Obstetric billing features

- [ ] **Pregnancy record** with first-visit date, EDD, gravida/para, risk factors
- [ ] **Antepartum visit counter** that tracks visits by provider and payer
- [ ] **Global package and component billing** through 2026 (`59400`/`59510` families, `59425`/`59426`, `59430`)
- [ ] **2027 maternity code support**: per-encounter prenatal E/M, labor management `59080`–`59083`, delivery `59431`/`59432`/`59502`/`59503`/`59504`
- [ ] A plan for **pregnancies spanning January 1, 2027**
- [ ] Alerts for **insurance changes** during pregnancy
- [ ] **Delivery claim edits**: `Z37`, `Z3A`, `O80` rules, VBAC code families

See our [2027 maternity transition guide](/resources/2027-maternity-transition) for what the system must support.

## Gynecology and procedure features

- [ ] Charge capture for **ultrasound** (`76801`–`76857`) with gestational-age checks
- [ ] **LARC device** charge capture tied to inventory
- [ ] **Surgical charge** workflow that waits for pathology when needed
- [ ] **Modifier support** for `25`, `59`/X-modifiers, `22`, `50`, global-period modifiers
- [ ] **Preventive + problem visit** billing on the same date

## Claims and clearinghouse

- [ ] Built-in or integrated **claim scrubber** with NCCI edits and custom rules
- [ ] Ability to add **OB/GYN- and payer-specific rules**
- [ ] **Clearinghouse** included or integrated — and its per-claim fees
- [ ] **Rejection work queue** visible to billing staff the same day
- [ ] **Corrected claims** with proper frequency codes
- [ ] **Secondary and tertiary** claim automation

## Payments and posting

- [ ] **ERA auto-posting** with exception handling
- [ ] **EFT enrollment** support
- [ ] **Underpayment detection** against loaded contract rates
- [ ] **Patient payments**: online, text-to-pay, card on file, payment plans
- [ ] Clear **adjustment codes** that separate contractual, denial and write-off adjustments

## Denials and A/R

- [ ] **Denial work queue** sortable by reason (CARC/RARC), payer, provider and dollar value
- [ ] **A/R aging** by payer and by claim status
- [ ] **Follow-up dates** and notes on each claim
- [ ] **Appeal letter templates** and attachment support

## Reporting

- [ ] **Net collection rate**, **days in A/R**, **A/R over 90 days** without spreadsheets
- [ ] **Denial reports** by reason and provider
- [ ] **Charge lag** report
- [ ] **Open pregnancies and unbilled deliveries** report
- [ ] **Provider productivity** (RVUs, visits, procedures)
- [ ] Scheduled reports emailed monthly

Our guide to [OB/GYN billing KPIs](/blog/obgyn-billing-kpis) explains which reports matter.

## Front desk and patient access

- [ ] **Real-time eligibility** checks from the schedule
- [ ] **Benefit details** for maternity, preventive and surgical services
- [ ] **Prior authorization tracking** linked to scheduled procedures
- [ ] **Patient cost estimates**
- [ ] **Patient portal** for forms, statements and payments

## Telehealth

- [ ] Integrated or supported **video visits**
- [ ] Correct **POS `02`/`10`** and **modifier `95`/`93`** handling by payer
- [ ] Support for **telemedicine E/M codes** where payers use them

See [modifier 95 vs 93 vs GT](/blog/modifier-95-vs-93-vs-gt-telehealth).

## Security and compliance

- [ ] Vendor signs a **Business Associate Agreement**
- [ ] **Multi-factor authentication** for all users
- [ ] **Role-based access** and individual user accounts
- [ ] **Audit logs** of access and changes
- [ ] Encryption in transit and at rest; documented backup and disaster recovery
- [ ] **Session timeouts** and automatic logoff

## Contract and implementation

- [ ] Total cost: subscription, per-provider fees, clearinghouse, statements, add-on modules, implementation, training
- [ ] Contract length, renewal terms and price increase limits
- [ ] **Data ownership** and export format if you leave
- [ ] Implementation timeline and who does what
- [ ] Training included, and support hours and response times
- [ ] References from **OB/GYN practices** of your size

## Questions that reveal the most

1. "Show me a patient who transferred in at 30 weeks and delivered by cesarean — how is she billed?"
2. "How will you handle a patient whose prenatal care starts in November 2026 and who delivers in March 2027?"
3. "Where does my biller see today's rejections?"
4. "Show me net collection rate by payer for last quarter."
5. "What will this cost us all-in for year one and year two?"

## Software is half the answer

Good software makes good billing possible; it doesn't do the billing. Practices still need skilled people to work denials, follow up on A/R and keep up with payer rules — in-house or through a partner. Read [RCM software vs. outsourced billing](/blog/obgyn-rcm-software-vs-outsourced-billing) for how to think about both.
