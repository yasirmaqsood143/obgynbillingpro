## Our story

OBGYNBillingPro grew out of more than eight years of hands-on medical billing work — much of it for women's health practices. Over that time we kept seeing the same thing: independent OB/GYN practices delivering excellent care while quietly losing revenue to billing problems that were entirely fixable.

Antepartum visits went unbilled when a patient transferred out. Global maternity claims sat unsubmitted for months after delivery. Hysterectomies were billed before the pathology report confirmed the uterine weight. Denials were resubmitted unchanged, again and again. And general-purpose billing companies, handling dozens of specialties at once, often didn't recognize any of these as problems at all.

So we focused. OB/GYN billing is complicated enough to deserve a team that does it every day — one that understands global maternity packages, gynecologic surgery coding, Chapter 15 diagnosis rules, and the payer policies that govern women's health. That's what we built.

## What we believe

### Accuracy over aggression

We code what the documentation supports — no more, no less. Undercoding costs you revenue; overcoding creates compliance risk. Neither is acceptable. When documentation doesn't support the code a provider expects, we explain why and how to document it next time.

### Honesty over hype

You won't find inflated statistics, invented testimonials or certifications we don't hold on our website. We tell prospective clients plainly what we can do, what we can't, and what we don't yet know until we've seen their data. That's also why we start with a free audit: you should see real findings from your own claims before deciding anything.

### People, not shortcuts

Every claim is coded, every denial is worked and every appeal is written by experienced billing professionals who read the documentation and the payer's policy. Technology helps us work efficiently — claim scrubbers, clearinghouse tools, payer portals — but judgment stays with people.

### Your practice, your data

You keep ownership of your systems, your data and your payer contracts. We work inside your practice management system with individual user accounts, and if you ever decide to move on, the transition is clean.

## What makes OB/GYN billing different

Most medical specialties bill visit by visit. An OB/GYN practice runs three very different billing models at once:

1. **Obstetric care**, billed through 2026 as global packages that span a whole pregnancy, and — starting January 1, 2027 — by phase of care under the new CPT maternity codes.
2. **Gynecologic surgery**, with high-value claims, prior authorizations, NCCI edits and 90-day global periods.
3. **Office gynecology and preventive care**, including well-woman visits, problem visits, ultrasounds, contraception and in-office procedures.

Each model has its own rules and its own ways to lose money. Our team is organized around all three, and around the specialties within women's health: [maternal-fetal and global maternity billing](/global-maternity-billing), [gynecologic surgery](/gyn-surgery-coding), [reproductive endocrinology and infertility](/infertility-rei-billing), and [urogynecology](/urogynecology-billing).

## What a typical month looks like with us

- **Every business day:** charges reviewed and coded, clean claims submitted, rejections fixed, payments posted.
- **Within two business days of a denial:** the denial is categorized and corrected, reconsidered or appealed.
- **Every week:** unpaid claims followed up by age and value; surgical claims waiting on pathology reviewed; eligibility checked for upcoming visits.
- **Every month:** a written report covering collections, net collection rate, A/R aging, denial reasons, charge lag and open pregnancies — and a call to walk through it, including what we changed and why.
- **Whenever rules change:** payer policy updates, annual CPT and ICD-10-CM updates and the 2027 maternity transition are built into your billing before they cause denials.
