## What are ICD-10 O codes?

Chapter 15 of ICD-10-CM — **Pregnancy, Childbirth and the Puerperium** — contains the codes in the range `O00`–`O9A`. These are the "O codes," and they're used on the mother's record for any condition that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth or the puerperium (the six weeks following delivery), or that complicates the pregnancy.

O codes are different from most ICD-10-CM codes in three important ways:

1. **They take sequencing priority.** Chapter 15 codes are listed before codes from other chapters.
2. **They often require a trimester character** that reflects the patient's gestational age on the date of the encounter.
3. **Some require a seventh character to identify the fetus** in multiple gestations.

They're also used **only on the maternal record** — never on the newborn's record.

## O code categories at a glance

| Range | Block |
|---|---|
| `O00`–`O08` | Pregnancy with abortive outcome (ectopic, molar, miscarriage, termination) |
| `O09` | Supervision of high-risk pregnancy |
| `O10`–`O16` | Edema, proteinuria and hypertensive disorders (including pre-eclampsia) |
| `O20`–`O29` | Other maternal disorders predominantly related to pregnancy (including gestational diabetes `O24.4-`) |
| `O30`–`O48` | Maternal care related to the fetus and amniotic cavity and possible delivery problems (multiples, malpresentation, previous cesarean `O34.21-`, fetal problems, post-term `O48`) |
| `O60`–`O77` | Complications of labor and delivery |
| `O80`–`O82` | Encounter for delivery (`O80` uncomplicated vaginal, `O82` cesarean without indication) |
| `O85`–`O92` | Complications predominantly related to the puerperium |
| `O94`–`O9A` | Other obstetric conditions, including `O99.-` other maternal diseases complicating pregnancy |

## Sequencing rules: the core principles

### 1. Chapter 15 codes come first

When a patient is pregnant and the condition affects or is affected by the pregnancy, the O code is sequenced before codes from other chapters. Codes from other chapters can follow to add specificity — for example, `O99.21-` (obesity complicating pregnancy) followed by the appropriate `E66.-` code to specify the type of obesity.

### 2. Assume the condition affects the pregnancy — unless documented otherwise

If a pregnant patient is seen for a condition, it's coded as complicating the pregnancy unless the provider explicitly documents that it is **not** affecting the pregnancy. In that case, the condition is sequenced first and `Z33.1` (pregnant state, incidental) is added.

### 3. Routine prenatal visits: Z34 vs O09

- **Normal pregnancy, no complications:** `Z34.0-` (first pregnancy) or `Z34.8-` (other normal pregnancy) as the first-listed code. Not used with any O code.
- **High-risk pregnancy:** `O09.-` supervision codes first-listed for routine prenatal visits — for example `O09.51-` (elderly primigravida) or `O09.81-` (pregnancy resulting from assisted reproductive technology). Other O codes may be added as secondary codes.

### 4. Prenatal visits for complications

When a visit is for a complication, that complication is the first-listed diagnosis. If more than one complication is addressed, the one that's the main reason for the visit goes first; when they're equal, any may be sequenced first.

### 5. Delivery encounters

The principal diagnosis is the **main circumstance or complication of the delivery**. For a cesarean delivery, it's the condition responsible for the cesarean — unless the reason for admission was unrelated to the condition that led to the cesarean.

## Multiple O codes: a worked example

A patient at 32 weeks with gestational diabetes controlled by insulin and mild pre-eclampsia is seen primarily for blood pressure management. Both conditions are addressed.

1. `O14.03` — mild to moderate pre-eclampsia, third trimester (main reason for visit)
2. `O24.414` — gestational diabetes mellitus in pregnancy, insulin controlled
3. `Z3A.32` — 32 weeks gestation

Note that `Z79.4` (long-term use of insulin) is **not** added with gestational diabetes codes from subcategory `O24.4-` — the insulin control is already captured in the O code. If the same patient's next visit focuses on adjusting her insulin, the gestational diabetes code moves to the first position.

## Trimester characters

Most O codes end with a character for trimester, based on gestational age **on the date of the encounter**:

- **First trimester:** less than 14 weeks 0 days
- **Second trimester:** 14 weeks 0 days to less than 28 weeks 0 days
- **Third trimester:** 28 weeks 0 days until delivery

Some codes also include an "in childbirth" or "in the puerperium" option. For an inpatient admission that spans more than one trimester, the trimester at the time of admission is used. Codes for conditions that only occur in certain trimesters don't offer all options.

## Seventh character for fetus identification

In multiple gestations, certain categories — including `O31`, `O32`, `O33.3`–`O33.6`, `O35`, `O36`, `O40`, `O41`, `O60.1`–`O60.2`, `O64` and `O69` — require a seventh character to identify which fetus the condition applies to: `1` through `9` for each fetus, or `0` for a single gestation or when the fetus can't be identified.

## Required companion Z codes

- **`Z3A.-` weeks of gestation** — added with Chapter 15 codes on the maternal record to report gestational age. Not used for pregnancies with abortive outcomes (`O00`–`O08`), elective termination, or postpartum conditions.
- **`Z37.-` outcome of delivery** — required on every maternal delivery record (for example `Z37.0`, single live birth). Never on the newborn record.
- **`Z39.-` postpartum encounters** — `Z39.2` for routine postpartum follow-up, `Z39.1` for care of a lactating mother.
- **`Z32.0-` pregnancy test** — `Z32.00` unconfirmed, `Z32.01` positive, `Z32.02` negative. In the outpatient setting, don't code a "rule out pregnancy" as confirmed; code the result or the presenting symptoms.

## Using O80 correctly

`O80` (encounter for full-term uncomplicated delivery) applies only when all of these are true: full-term, single liveborn infant, vaginal delivery, and no complications during the delivery admission. It's always the principal diagnosis, is paired only with `Z37.0`, and can't be used with any other Chapter 15 code. A complication earlier in the pregnancy that has resolved by the delivery admission doesn't prevent its use.

## Common O-code errors on OB claims

- `Z34.-` combined with O codes
- Missing `Z37` on the delivery claim
- Wrong trimester character because the gestational age wasn't checked on the date of service
- `O80` used with other O codes, or for a cesarean delivery
- Missing seventh character for multiple gestations
- Previous cesarean coded without the scar type (`O34.211` low transverse, `O34.212` vertical, `O34.218` other)
- Pre-existing conditions coded with gestational codes (for example `O13` gestational hypertension for a patient with chronic hypertension, which belongs in `O10`)
